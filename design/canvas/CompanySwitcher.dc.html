Top bar · shared

Company and month switcher

The company is chosen in the top bar, left of the month. Every page works on the company chosen; only the Overview can show all companies, and HR › Staff can list them read only. Each frame below is the real top bar at its width beside the side menu, and it works: open the menus, use the keys, pick a company.

Closed · AISB on Payments › Payments register

The button names the company by its code and how its books are kept: "AISB · Kept in SQL Account". The month reads "October 2026", without "payroll".

No company yet

Before the first company exists, the button reads "No company yet" and its menu holds one item, "Add your first company", which opens Admin › Setup › Companies. This replaces the box at the foot of the old side menu.

Open · no All companies (any page but the Overview)

Each company in use: code, full name, how its books are kept, and its Next up count (blue; red with the alert mark when one of its rows is overdue). The chosen one is ticked. AISB 11 (3 overdue), AI 5 (1 overdue).

Open on the Overview · All companies first

All companies is offered on the Overview (and read only on HR › Staff). Its count, 15, counts the rows shared by both companies once (the bank balance for the budget, the backup): 12 + 5 − 2.

Long list · four companies in use

The four-company variant: AIL (Kept in SQL Account) and AIP (Kept here) have 2 rows each, none overdue. Stopped companies are never listed: OLD · Former Trading Sdn Bhd (stopped 30 Jun 2025) and TEST · Training company (stopped 21 Jul 2026).

HR › Staff · All companies (read only)

The staff list can show every company's staff at once, read only: 14 in employment. To add or change a record, pick its company.

Switch, step 1 · on Accounting › SQL import, pick AI

AI is Kept here, so it has no SQL import page. Pick AI in this frame to see step 2 happen. Picking a company that has the page keeps you on it (Budget, Payments register, Books and every HR page exist for all).

Never a blank or refused page: you land on AI's Overview and one polite toast says why. Dark uses the same tokens as the rest of the bar. Control borders are 3:1 or more in both themes (light #7C8799, dark #7A89A3); every text is 4.5:1 or more.

Dark · month menu

Current is the link colour; Set up is a stronger grey than the other statuses.

Keyboard

↓ Enter SpaceOn the company or month button: opens the menu with focus on the chosen item. ↑ opens it on the last item. ↓ ↑Move through the items; the ends wrap round. Home EndFirst or last item. A … ZType-ahead: letters typed within 0.6 s jump to the first item that starts with them ("ai" lands on AI, "ail" on AIL; "s" lands on September). Pressing the same letter again moves on to the next match. Enter SpacePicks the item. The menu closes and focus goes back to its button. EscCloses the menu; focus goes back to its button. On the toast, closes the toast and focus goes to the company button. TabCloses the menu and moves on. A click outside also closes it.

Menus are role menu with menuitemradio items, so a screen reader hears which company and month are chosen. Each company item reads as, for example, "AISB, AISB Sdn Bhd, Kept in SQL Account, 11 to do, 3 overdue". Under reduced motion the menus and the toast appear and leave at once.

Rules

  • One company switcher, in the top bar left of the month. It replaces the AISB / AI control that sat on 19 pages.
  • A company is Kept in SQL Account or Kept here, and runs payroll in this app or not. These are settings on Admin › Setup › Companies; no company is treated differently by its code.
  • Pages used by some companies only: SQL import and Matching words (Kept in SQL Account), Sales, Purchases, Journals and Period close (Kept here), Payroll settings (runs payroll in this app). HR › Payroll shows for every company, with the Workbook check tab for all.
  • The breadcrumb names the five areas: Overview, HR, Payments, Accounting, Admin.
  • The page address keeps company, month and page, so Back and Refresh keep your place (build plan, slice 0).
