The legal minimums are the entitlement (Employment Act 1955, as at Nov 2026): annual leave 8, 12 or 16 days by years of service. By law, untaken annual leave carries into the next year, to take within 12 months. Nothing above the law is given until the owner sets it on Leave types and policy; leave beyond a person's legal days is a warning only, never refused.
