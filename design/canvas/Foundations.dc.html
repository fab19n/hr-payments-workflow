Dates and money

One rule each, used everywhere: boards, toasts, the trail, the search.

Count days only inside one week either side of today. Further out, show the date. Always give the exact date on hover, or on tap (it opens a why note). Shown here as on Wednesday 18 November 2026.

Due dateShown asExact date, on hover

Other dates: 15 Nov in the current year, 15 Nov 2025 in any other. Page date line: Wednesday 18 November 2026. Months: October 2026. Time: 3:42 pm. A period: 1 to 31 Oct.

Full figures, with cents, in tables, dialogs and statements. Rounded figures only on Overview tiles. Never abbreviated (no k or m), always RM first (except Books report tables, see Accounting figures), thousands with a comma, right-aligned in tabular figures.

Accounting figures

Books pages only: trial balance, ledger, statements, customers and suppliers, statutory. They follow the app, not the payment tables.
No RM in report tables. The heading or the page says the currency; a figure is just 1,234.56. Payment tables and the Overview keep RM first.
A balance reads x Dr or x Cr. In a statement, a figure the other way round from its group goes in brackets: (103,852.70).
— means not known. For example SQL’s figure when its trial balance has not been fetched. It never means zero.
Blank for zero in a movement column. A nil closing balance is also blank in the trial balance’s Debit and Credit cells. Where one Balance column is shown, as in the ledger and this example, a nil balance is written 0.00, so a blank there is never read as nil.
New work. Proposed: one date style in report headings, sentences and ledger rows, 31 Oct, with the year (31 Oct 2025) when it is not the current one. Until that is built the Books boards keep what the app does today: the long form in report headings (Trial balance, 1 October 2026 to 31 October 2026), dd/mm/yyyy in report notes and Excel (as at 31/10/2026), and 31 Oct in some sentences.
