The rules
Position. Desktop: bottom right, 16 px in from the edges. Phone: bottom centre, above the tab bar. Never over the dialog's Save row.
Stack. At most three, newest at the bottom. A fourth makes the oldest one that isn't an error leave.
Stays. 4 s. 8 s when it carries Undo or a way on. 10 s for a long result. The timer pauses while the toast is hovered or has focus, and starts again when it lets go.
Errors stay until dismissed, and are read out at once (role="alert"). Everything else is polite (status).
Long result. Several short lines, one fact each: what was done, what was left alone, where it went. If the person needs it later, it also goes in an inline result block.
Progress shows a thin bar and becomes the success toast in place. Only for work longer than 2 s. New work: the app shows work on the button today.
Undo only on saves and stops, never on a removal or filing (they have a confirm). Undoing is kept as new work in the audit trail: the change and the undo are two entries, and the toast says so. New work: needs a revert path in the app.
Company switch. Picking a company that doesn't use the page you are on opens that company's Overview and says why in one polite toast. 4 s.
Motion. Rises 8 px over 240 ms; leaves in 180 ms; the stack doesn't jump.
