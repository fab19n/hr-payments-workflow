The three books bookmarks sit at the top level, between Bank reconciliation and the six HR and approval pages that follow them (pages 7 to 12), then the documents. With Include payslips on, the payslips come after the HR pages and the documents start later. Page numbers are for this sample month.

How the three pages read

  • The page head is the company name, the month and the page title. The body is plain text in the pack's usual small type, so a line wraps and nothing is a table. The pages here are drawn at print size (15 pt head, 9 pt body, 10 mm margins on A4), so zoom in to read them; lines wrap where the PDF wraps them.
  • SQL books check says Done, Not done or Not checked here, then the one sentence that explains it, then the standing paragraph that says SQL stays the books.
  • Statutory payables check starts with the summary, then one line for each statutory account for the payroll month, then any notes and mispostings.
  • Trial balance against SQL starts with one of four verdicts, then the file it was compared with and the result. Each account that differs gets its own line.
  • For a company that is Kept here the first and third pages say so and the second says nothing is set up. The pages are still printed, so the PDF has the same shape for every company.
