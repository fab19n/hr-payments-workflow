When the 60-and-over rates start

With the payroll month after the month of the 60th birthday, whatever the day: someone who turns 60 in October is paid at the 60-and-over rates from November. SOCSO and EIS change in the same month as EPF.

Common payroll practice, not a published rule. KWSP's Third Schedule only says the rates change when someone reaches 60, and PERKESO ties SOCSO and EIS to reaching 60; neither says which month. Ask KWSP (03-8922 6000) and PERKESO (1-300-22-8000), then change this if they say otherwise.

PCB isn't in this table: the Workbook check doesn't check PCB amounts. LHDN's computerised method is kept per year when payroll is run here.
