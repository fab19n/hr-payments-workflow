SQL import

Fetch (or paste) each SQL listing for the month: Cash Book, Receipt Voucher, Payment Voucher, Journal and Customer Invoice. Payments and receipts are matched to the payments in HR Payments; customer invoices are filed against their checklist item of their own month and linked to the money in they are for, and a payroll journal against Salary PVs in SQL. A document that appears in two listings is only kept once, so overlap is fine. The e-Invoices this fetch reads, and the printed invoices, have their own page: e-Invoices.

Agrinexus International is kept here, so there is nothing to import from SQL Account. Switch to a company that is Kept in SQL Account to import its SQL listings.

Paste a listing instead

In SQL: open the listing, then the arrow beside the print button, Export to Microsoft Excel Workbook, and save it in SQL Drive on SQL Connect › Download. It reads the Cash Book, Payment Voucher, Official Receipt, Journal Voucher, Customer Invoice, Sales Invoice, Customer Payment, Supplier Invoice and Supplier Payment listings (not their GST layouts). It also reads SQL’s GL Journal of Transaction (the account lines of every document), GL Ledger (Local - Ledger - Detail, Level 1) and Trial Balance (This Month vs This Year with Opening): export them for the same dates as the listings, and the lines are kept and checked against SQL’s own figures. It also reads a copy of SQL’s MyInvois Transaction screen: click its grid, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, paste it into a new Excel workbook with its headings and save it in the same folder under any name.
