Printed bank reconciliation statement

What opens when you press Print the reconciliation statement on Bank and reconciliation. One statement per bank account and month, in the standard layout: the bank balance, the items that make up the difference, and the book balance it comes to. Then the book entries where no money moved, the totals check, the check against SQL's books for a company Kept in SQL Account, and Prepared by and Reviewed by with the sign-off note. The signed copy is uploaded like an approval form and filed against the month; it is the detail behind part 4 of the Month review.
