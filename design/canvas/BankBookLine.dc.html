Book a statement line

A line that is only on the statement, such as a bank charge or bank profit, is booked from the line itself after checking it. Money out posts a payment voucher, money in an official receipt. It is numbered when it is posted, linked to the line, and never edited afterwards.

Book a statement line is for companies kept here

AISB is Kept in SQL Account. Explain the line as a bank charge on the review, enter it in SQL Account (Dr 900-010 Bank charges, Cr 310-000 Maybank current), then fetch SQL again so the books agree with the bank.
