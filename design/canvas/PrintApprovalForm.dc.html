Printed approval form

What Print approval form makes: one or more requests on an A4 form, in English, with the form's code, its keyed check and a QR holding both, the requests and their total, and a block for each of the company's approvers on the print date. Any one signature approves this form. The signed form is uploaded on Payments › Approvals or photographed with the phone.

The page is printed, not shown on screen. It has no menu or top bar: A4 portrait at full size (210 × 297 mm), black and grey on white, body text at 9 pt, "Page x of y" on every page and the header repeated on page 2. Only the frame and the notes around it belong to the app. The QR drawn here is a stand-in pattern and can't be scanned.

What Print approval form says, and what is printed: nothing

Nothing was printed
AIP has no approvers yet, so there is no one to print on the form. Approval forms can't be printed until a company has an approver. Add the director who signs for AIP, then print again.
Add an approver
The request stays under To print on Payments › Approvals. No form code is used up, nothing is saved and nothing goes in the audit trail. Approval never blocks paying: the request can still be paid from Payments › To pay. Once paid, it reads Paid without approval on file until a signed form is uploaded. The same refusal shows when the key behind the keyed check hasn't been made yet: "Approval forms can be printed once there is a key." The key is made on Setup › HR › Approvers.
