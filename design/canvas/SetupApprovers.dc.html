Who may sign the printed approval forms. Up to 3 for each company, and any one signature approves a form. A form prints the company's approvers as they are on the day, and old forms keep the names as they were. People with the Approver right on Users and access are picked from this list.

Add a company first.

Approvers belong to a company. Once one is added, its approvers are set up here.
