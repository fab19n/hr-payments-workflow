Printed Month review sheet

What opens when you press Print the Month review on Payments › Approvals or on the Monthly checklist. One approver reviews the month and signs it: the payments register total, what was paid without approval on file, the approvals each approver gave, and the bank reconciliation. The signed sheet is uploaded like an approval form, and the checklist item Month reviewed ticks once it is on file. Signing it approves no payment.
