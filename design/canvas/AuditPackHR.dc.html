How these pages read

Declared, not verified
  • Plain text in the pack's small type, like the books pages: a head with the company, the month and the page title, then lines that wrap where the PDF wraps them. Drawn at print size, so zoom in to read.
  • Until logins exist, every name typed or chosen at the keyboard says "declared, not verified". A paper scan reads "Approver chosen by <name> from a paper scan"; a digitally signed PDF says the signer's name was matched.
  • No IC number, bank account, address or medical detail on these pages, and pay only as totals per authority. Payslips are added only when Include payslips is on.
  • Every company gets the six pages, so every pack has the same shape. A company that doesn't run payroll in the app says so on the HR summary.
