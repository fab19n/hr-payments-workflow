What happens next

  1. Save the draft. It gets its claim number.
  2. Print it on an approval form, alone or with other drafts. Approvals keeps the form.
  3. Any one of AISB's approvers signs it; the signed form is uploaded.
  4. Approved, it waits on To pay and is paid on its own, never in the payroll run.
  5. Its payment carries the receipts, so its documents are complete.
