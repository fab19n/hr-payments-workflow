Template

The rules only: payment types, document types and the documents each needs, a starter checklist, the joiner and leaver reminders, the matching words and the general settings. No company, bank account, customer, supplier or person.

Companies added in the other install start with it.

Everything

The template, plus the companies, their bank accounts with the account numbers, charts of accounts, statutory accounts, customers and suppliers, each company’s checklist, what its printed invoices need (address, bank details, logo and signature), the printed company names confirmed, and each company’s HR settings: leave types and policy, public holidays, claim types and payroll settings. Keep the file safe.

The file then holds personal data ({{personalWhat}}). Keep it as safe as a backup and delete it once it has been imported.
Never in the file: staff records, with their leave, claims, advances and payroll, and the secret behind the approval forms’ keyed check. The other install makes its own secret, so a form printed here can’t be matched there.
